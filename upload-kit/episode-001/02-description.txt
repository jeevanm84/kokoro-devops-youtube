Is Python interpreted or compiled? Both. This video shows, step by step, what CPython does between pressing Enter and seeing output: tokenizing, parsing to an AST, compiling to bytecode, executing on a stack-based virtual machine, how names bind to objects (reference counting), and how call frames build the call stack.

Everything shown was checked against a real CPython 3.12 interpreter. Details differ in other implementations (e.g. PyPy) and versions.
